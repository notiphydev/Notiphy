Notiphy Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Information Collection and Use

Notiphy is a local user interface customization utility. The application requests access to the Android Notification Listener Service and Storage permissions strictly to detect incoming user alerts and render custom animated overlay graphics on the user's local device screen.

2. Zero Data Transmission (Local Environment)

Notiphy is built entirely on a serverless architecture. All notification processing, text variable mapping, and custom media asset caching happen locally on the user's physical device. Absolutely zero personal data, message content, notification history, or private analytics are collected, tracked, stored, or transmitted to external servers or any third parties.

3. Advertising and Monetization

The application utilizes Google AdMob to serve banner and rewarded video advertisements. Google AdMob may collect device identifiers locally to deliver relevant test and production ad units.

4. Data Security

Because no data ever leaves your device, your private notifications and messages are completely protected by your phone's native Android security sandbox encryption boundaries.
