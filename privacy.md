NOTIPHY — PRIVACY POLICY
Last Updated: August 2026

1. INFORMATION COLLECTION AND USE
Notiphy is a local user interface customization utility. The app requests access to the Android Notification Listener Service and Storage permissions strictly to detect incoming user alerts and render custom animated overlay graphics on the user's local device screen.

2. ZERO DATA TRANSMISSION (LOCAL ENVIRONMENT)
Notiphy is built on a serverless architecture. All notification processing, text variable mapping, and custom media asset caching happen entirely on the user's local physical device. Absolutely zero personal data, message content, notification history, or private analytics are collected, tracked, stored, or transmitted to any external servers or third parties.

3. ADVERTISING AND MONETIZATION
The application utilizes Google AdMob to serve banner and rewarded video advertisements. Google AdMob may collect device identifiers locally to deliver relevant test and production ad units. 

4. DATA SECURITY
Because no data ever leaves your device, your private notifications and messages are completely protected by your phone's native Android security sandbox encryption boundaries.
